How the columns are counted
Clean kills
Spells that destroy or exile the creature unconditionally, at any size.
Conditional
Sweepers, and spells with a board-state condition such as "attacking creature".
Edicts
Sacrifice effects. They get past hexproof and protection, but the opponent picks what dies.
Soft
Bounce, tap, steal, or neutralize. Answers the creature without killing it. The toggles above can promote neutralize and steal to kills.
Exile
The clean kills that exile, so they also beat indestructible, recursion, and death triggers.
Artifact and enchantment removal
Counts only against creatures that also have that type, and is labelled "hits it as an artifact" or "as an enchantment" in the creature's spell list.
Counterspells
Listed in Review and in Removal by colour, but never counted per creature. They answer the spell on the stack, not the creature on the board.
*
On a P/T means the value was assumed, using the setting above.
