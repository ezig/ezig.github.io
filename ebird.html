How to get your eBird data
  1. Sign in at ebird.org/downloadMyData (also under My eBird → Download My Data).
  2. Click Submit. eBird will email you a link, usually within a few minutes.
  3. Download the zip from that email and unzip it to get MyEBirdData.csv.
  4. Drop that file onto this page, or use the Load button.
Your file never leaves your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
